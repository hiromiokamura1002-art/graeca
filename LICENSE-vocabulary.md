# New Testament lemma frequency list — CC BY-SA 3.0

`nt-frequency.csv` is a derived work of **MorphGNT: SBLGNT Edition** (James Tauber et al.), https://github.com/morphgnt/sblgnt, whose lemma and morphological-analysis layer is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 Unported License (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/). The underlying text is the SBL Greek New Testament (© 2010 Society of Biblical Literature and Logos Bible Software, CC BY 4.0).

What was done for the app **ギリシャ語ドリル** (Greek Drill): every token of the 27 books was tallied by lemma and part of speech; the columns give the lemma, MorphGNT part-of-speech code, rank and count in the whole New Testament, and rank and count within the Gospel of John.

This derived list is published under the same license, CC BY-SA 3.0. The rest of the app (code, questions, explanations, Japanese glosses, readings) is not covered by this license.
